`earthrise.jpg` — "Earthrise," photographed by Apollo 8 astronaut Bill Anders on December 24,
1968 (NASA catalog id `AS08-14-2383`). Public domain: NASA material is not protected by
copyright unless otherwise noted (17 U.S.C. § 105). Sourced from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA-Apollo8-Dec24-Earthrise.jpg), resized and
re-compressed to 1600x1600 JPEG for web use -- no other changes made.

Used as the `app.py` hero background. Thematically apt beyond just "space imagery": it's the
single most famous photograph associated with the Apollo program this corpus documents, and
several `eval/qa_set.jsonl` questions are about this exact photo.
